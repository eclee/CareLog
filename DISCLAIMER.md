# Medical and Privacy Disclaimer

CareLog assists with household care reporting and communication. It does not
provide diagnosis, treatment advice, medication orders, emergency monitoring,
or guaranteed message delivery. Threshold alerts and dashboard summaries are
informational and may be delayed, incomplete, or affected by incorrect input.

Deployers are responsible for local privacy, labor, health-care, record-
retention, consent, and cybersecurity requirements. Use professional medical
services and emergency channels whenever a person's condition may require
urgent attention.
