# Code of Conduct

CareLog contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss ideas
rather than personalities, and protect the dignity and privacy of older adults,
caregivers, and families represented in examples or tests.

Harassment, discrimination, publication of personal health information, and
hostile or degrading language are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to
keep the project safe and constructive.

When reporting a problem, use synthetic data and anonymized screenshots. A
technical demonstration is never a justification for exposing a real person's
health record or image.
